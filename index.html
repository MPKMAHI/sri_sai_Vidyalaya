About Our School

Nurturing Excellence Since 2011

Our History

Founded in 2011, Srisai Vidyalaya High School has been at the forefront of quality education for over a decade. What started as a modest campus with 50 students has blossomed into a premier educational hub, offering world-class infrastructure and holistic learning approaches.

Our Mission

To cultivate academic excellence, foster creativity, and instill strong ethical values in students, enabling them to become responsible global citizens.

Our Vision

To be a leading global educational institution recognized for nurturing innovative thinkers, outstanding leaders, and lifelong learners.
